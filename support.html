Support

Need help?

Open a support request on GitHub Issues with your macOS version and a short description of the issue.

Common questions

  • Where is the app? Days Away lives in the macOS menu bar. Look for the calendar icon near the top-right of your screen.
  • Where is my data stored? Events and settings are stored locally on your Mac in Application Support.
  • Does it sync? No. Days Away is local-only.
  • Can I disable notifications? Yes. Open Settings from the popover and turn Notifications off.
