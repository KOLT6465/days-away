Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Days Away is designed to be private and local.

Data Collection

Days Away does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data.

Local Storage

Events and preferences are stored locally on your Mac. This data stays on your device unless you choose to back up, copy, or sync your Mac through tools outside Days Away.

Network Access

Days Away does not use internet services, analytics, advertising SDKs, accounts, or external APIs.

Notifications

If notifications are enabled, Days Away schedules local macOS notifications on your Mac. Notification data is not sent to the developer.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use GitHub Issues.
